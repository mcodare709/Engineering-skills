# Research

## Explain a Paper

- Core mechanism
- Architecture flow
- Design motivation
- Strengths
- Limits and assumptions
- Relevance to current task

Do not restate abstract. Inspect objective, mechanism, evidence, and failure cases.

## Design an Experiment

```text
Hypothesis:
Method:
Baselines:
Ablations:
Datasets and split:
Metrics and statistics:
Expected contribution:
Failure cases:
Reviewer concerns:
```

## Baselines

Include simple baseline, closest method, and current strong methods verified at execution time. Match data, resolution, training budget, metric implementation, and test-time processing.

## Novelty Check

- More than known modules combined?
- Each claimed contribution isolated?
- Gain larger than run variance?
- Extra compute, data, supervision, or preprocessing disclosed?
- Cross-dataset or real-world generalization tested?
- Nearest work compared directly?

Separate paper claims, official-code results, and independent reproduction.
